How it works

One job. One thread. Every station.

Follow a single order through today’s core modules. Each step picks up exactly where the last one stopped, and every new module joins the same thread.

  1. 01

    Enquiry

    Drop in the customer's STEP file. ToolroomOS reads every part, works out sizes and shapes, builds the bill of materials and prepares the quotation. Save it as a PDF named after the quote ref.

  2. 02

    Design

    Sort parts into your categories, assign materials and item codes, and review each one in 3D with its actual and stock dimensions drawn on the box. Changes save as you go.

  3. 03

    Purchase

    Stock sizes become purchase orders by vendor. Receipts are booked against each line, so production knows exactly what material is in the building.

  4. 04

    Production

    Plan every operation, in-house machine or outside vendor, on a board or a table. Each team finishes its own category, and machine load is shown as you pick.

  5. 05

    Dispatch

    Raise GST-format delivery challans and pickup requests with addresses, HSN and value, then track each part at every vendor until it comes back complete.

Security & governance

Your drawings and prices stay yours.

Customer models, rates and vendor terms are the most sensitive data a toolroom has. ToolroomOS is built so each company sees only its own, and each person sees only the apps they are given.

Consistent documents

Every quote, PO and challan carries the same company details and numbers itself in sequence, set once in settings.

Company-level isolation

Every record belongs to a company, and every request is checked against the company in the user's session, on the server.

Invite-only access

Admins invite people, choose which apps they can open and see when each person last signed in.

Encrypted end to end

HTTPS everywhere, a managed database encrypted at rest, and desktop secrets stored encrypted on disk.

Separate test and live systems

Every change runs in a separate test environment against test data before it reaches your live system.

Nothing lost mid-plan

Plans and reviews save as you work, with a clear "All changes saved" status and time stamp.

FAQ

Questions buyers ask.

Anything else? Ask us directly.

Who is ToolroomOS for?

Mould makers, die and press-tool makers, mouldbase manufacturers and precision job shops: any toolroom that builds one-off parts from drawings and sends work to outside vendors.

Do we have to install anything?

No. ToolroomOS runs in the browser. Enquiry is also available as a Windows desktop app for heavy STEP files.

Can we start with one module?

Yes. Each module works on its own and shares the same data, so you can add the rest when you are ready.

We run more than one company. Does that work?

Yes. Each company keeps its own data, settings, numbering and team, and people who work across companies switch between them from the same account.

Is our data kept apart from other customers?

Yes. Every record is tied to its company, and the server checks the signed-in company on every request.

Are delivery challans GST compliant?

Challans follow the job-work format: consignor and consignee GSTIN, HSN, value, place of supply, transport details as GST regulations require, and numbering per financial year.

How is it priced?

By the modules and team size you need. Book a demo and we will send a quote for your shop.
